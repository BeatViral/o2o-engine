## Expansion Rule

Each app must have:
- industry-specific blind spots
- industry-specific workflows and cadence
- industry-specific risk language
- one weak-input demo that creates a jaw-drop diagnosis moment

## Sequencing Rule

Only expand after the current vertical proves:
- repeat weekly usage
- testimonial pull
- clear willingness to pay
- defensible blind-spot outcomes
