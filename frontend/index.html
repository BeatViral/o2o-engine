Blind Spot Diagnosis

Pre-search signals O2O surfaces before the market punishes the brief

  • You are hiring for activities, not outcomes.
  • You said "strong closer," but this role may require complex-cycle discipline, not transactional sales charm.
  • The salary range may attract the wrong level of candidate.
  • The candidate who looks right on paper may fail because the brief does not test for the real failure mode.
  • The best candidate is probably passive and not applying to job ads.

Output Sample

One sample output from the 16-output package.

This is one example only. You still get the full 16 outputs listed above.

Example input

"I'm a recruiter. Here's my job ad. We're looking for a Senior Account Executive for a B2B SaaS company, 5+ years experience, strong closer, base $120k. How do I find the best candidates?"

Example output

"O2O found 5 blind spots in this brief. Here is one high-impact extract from the system output."

  • Blind spot: The brief asks for a "strong closer" but this company actually needs an enterprise-cycle builder who can handle multi-threaded 6-9 month deals.
  • Corrected search thesis: Target AEs who moved from mid-market into enterprise complexity and can prove pipeline discipline, not just charisma.
  • Candidate persona: 4-7 years B2B SaaS, has closed $80k-$250k ACV deals, uses MEDDICC consistently, and can show stage-conversion metrics from CRM.
  • Boolean string: ("Account Executive" OR "Enterprise AE") AND (SaaS OR "B2B software") AND (MEDDICC OR "complex sales") AND ("new logo" OR hunter) AND (enterprise OR "multi-stakeholder")
  • Screening rubric sample: Pipeline discipline 40% (evidence of stage hygiene), complex-cycle execution 35% (multi-threaded deal examples), stakeholder influence 25% (cross-functional deal alignment).
Outreach message sample

"Hi Sarah, your move from mid-market into enterprise at X caught my eye. We're running a search for a Senior AE role where multi-stakeholder selling and CRM discipline matter more than pure volume. If you're open to a short confidential call, I can share the success profile."

21-day sprint sample
  • Days 1-7: Calibrate success profile, build 80-target list, launch first outreach wave.
  • Days 8-14: Screen first responses with rubric, tighten persona filters, run second wave.
  • Days 15-21: Present scored shortlist, run structured interviews, deliver final recommendation notes.
