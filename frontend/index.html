Live Demo Scenario

Senior Account Executive - B2B SaaS - $120k base - strong closer

1. Input

"I'm a recruiter. Here's my job ad. We're looking for a Senior Account Executive for a B2B SaaS company, 5+ years experience, strong closer, base $120k. How do I find the best candidates?"

2. O2O Diagnosis

"You are not just hiring a strong closer. You are deciding whether this company needs a transactional closer, a complex-cycle enterprise seller, or a sales-culture rebuilder. Those are three different people. Your job ad currently blurs them."

"Your current screening will overvalue charm and under-test pipeline discipline, CRM hygiene, deal qualification, sales-cycle patience and cross-functional influence."

3. O2O Output

"Corrected search thesis: target B2B SaaS AEs who have moved from mid-market into enterprise complexity, show evidence of disciplined pipeline management, and are likely 18-24 months into their current role where growth has plateaued."

  • Hidden success profile + candidate persona
  • Passive search strategy + Boolean strings
  • Screening rubric + interview architecture + red flags
